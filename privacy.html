Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers Hopeful Herald Publisher ("the app"), a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Hopeful Herald to manage that one channel. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only with the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app may:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publishing schedule on the owner's channel;
  • read the owner's channel and video information;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel (for example views and watch time) to improve future videos;
  • read comments on the channel's videos and post comments as the channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure and improve their performance.
  • Aggregated performance figures may be kept in the owner's private project files. Login tokens are stored privately (encrypted secrets / the owner's own computer) and are never published.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube Analytics provides to the channel owner.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services, or through Google security settings. Stored data is deleted on request or when access is revoked.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through The Hopeful Herald on YouTube.
